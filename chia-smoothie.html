Banana Avocado Chia Smoothie

Half a banana for sweetness, a quarter of an avocado for creaminess, chia seeds, cinnamon and unsweetened soy milk. Thick, filling and naturally sweet without any honey or sugar.

Method

Four steps and one blender.

  1. 1

    Soak the chia

    Stir the chia seeds into the soy milk in the blender jug and leave for 10 minutes. The seeds swell and gel, so the smoothie turns out smooth instead of gritty. You can also soak them in the fridge overnight.

    10 minutes
  2. 2

    Add the fruit

    Add the banana, the avocado scooped from its skin, the cinnamon and a few ice cubes. A frozen banana makes it thicker and colder.

    1 minute
  3. 3

    Blend until silky

    Blend on high until completely smooth and creamy, with no green flecks of avocado left.

    About 1 minute
  4. 4

    Adjust and drink

    Too thick? Blend in a splash more soy milk. Taste before adding anything: a ripe banana is usually sweet enough. Drink it fresh, as chia keeps thickening as it sits.

    1 minute
Working toward 50 g of chia a day
  • 1 tablespoon of chia is about 12 g, so 50 g a day is roughly 4 tablespoons.
  • This smoothie takes up to 2 tablespoons comfortably (about 25 g); add a splash more soy milk if you double the chia. Use the rest in oats or yogurt, or have a second smoothie.
  • 50 g of chia carries around 17 g of fibre. Build up over a week or two and drink plenty of water through the day.
Why it suits an LDL-friendly diet
  • Chia gives soluble fibre and plant omega-3 (ALA).
  • Avocado swaps saturated fat for mostly monounsaturated fat.
  • Unsweetened soy milk adds soy protein with very little saturated fat.
  • No cream, no ice cream, no honey or sugar.

Two simpler variations

Same method: soak the chia, add everything else, blend until smooth.

Banana chia smoothie

Naturally sweet
  • 1 small ripe banana
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 200 ml unsweetened soy milk or low-fat milk
  • ¼ tsp cinnamon
  • A few ice cubes

Blend until smooth. No added sugar needed.

Avocado chia smoothie

Best pick for LDL
  • ½ ripe avocado
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 200 ml unsweetened soy milk
  • 1 tsp lemon or lime juice
  • A few mint leaves
  • Ice cubes

Blend until silky smooth. Add a little extra milk if it's too thick.
