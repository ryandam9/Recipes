Barley, Mushroom & Vegetable Stew

A filling one-pot stew with barley, mushrooms and chickpeas, all from fresh ingredients

Serves four · Stovetop or multicooker

Ingredients

  • Pearl barley1 cup
    rinsed until the water runs clear
  • Olive oil2 tbsp
  • Brown onion, chopped1
  • Garlic cloves, chopped3
  • Carrots, diced2
  • Celery sticks, diced2
  • Mushrooms, sliced250 g
  • Ripe tomatoes, chopped3
  • Cooked chickpeas1½ cups
    about ½ cup dried chickpeas, soaked and cooked
  • Water6 cups
    4½ cups if pressure cooking
  • Dried thyme1 tsp
  • Smoked paprika1 tsp
  • Bay leaves2
  • Salt1 tsp
  • Black pepper½ tsp
  • Baby spinach2 cups
  • Lemon1, juiced

The secret: let the mushrooms turn properly golden. That browning gives the stew its deep flavour without stock.

Method

  1. I.

    Soften the vegetables

    Heat the oil in a large pot on medium heat. Add onion, carrots and celery. Cook for about 7 minutes, stirring now and then, until soft.

  2. II.

    Brown the mushrooms

    Add mushrooms and garlic. Cook for about 5 minutes until the mushrooms shrink and turn golden.

  3. III.

    Add barley and spices

    Stir in barley, thyme and smoked paprika. Cook for 1 minute until the spices smell good.

  4. IV.

    Simmer

    Add tomatoes, 6 cups water, bay leaves, salt and pepper. Bring to the boil, then turn the heat low, put the lid on and simmer for about 45 minutes. Stir every 10 minutes.

    Check the barley before moving on.It's ready when it's soft but still a little chewy. If the stew gets too thick, add a splash of hot water.
  5. V.

    Finish

    Stir in chickpeas and spinach and cook for 3 minutes until the spinach wilts. Take out the bay leaves, add the lemon juice and taste for salt.

Good to know

In the multicooker: do steps I–III on Saute. Add tomatoes, only 4½ cups water, bay leaves, salt and pepper. Scrape the bottom, then cook on Manual, high pressure, 20–25 minutes. Let the pressure drop on its own, then do step V on Saute.

Hulled barley: works too, with a nuttier bite. It needs 15–20 minutes longer.

Leftovers: keeps 4 days in the fridge. Add a splash of water when reheating, as the barley soaks up liquid.

Time: about 1 hour on the stove, or 45 minutes in the multicooker.
