Avocado Toast

Crisp wholegrain sourdough, a garlic rub, chunky lemony avocado and a drizzle of olive oil. Simple, filling and good for your heart, with a few easy toppings to make it your own.

10 minTotal time
EasySkill level

Tick things off as you gather them.

Servings
2

Method

Five steps, a toaster and a fork.

  1. 1

    Toast the bread well

    Toast the sourdough until deep golden and properly crisp. A sturdy, crunchy base is what makes it great, so don't stop at pale.

    3–4 minutes
  2. 2

    Rub with garlic

    While the toast is hot, rub one side lightly with the cut garlic clove. The rough surface grates it and leaves a gentle flavour.

    30 seconds
  3. 3

    Mash the avocado

    Scoop the avocado into a bowl. Add the lemon juice, salt and pepper and mash with a fork, keeping it chunky. Taste and add more lemon or salt if needed.

    1 minute
  4. 4

    Spread and top

    Pile the avocado onto the toast right to the edges. Add the cherry tomatoes, scatter over the seeds and chilli flakes, and finish with a drizzle of extra virgin olive oil and a few coriander leaves.

    1 minute
  5. 5

    Make it a meal (optional)

    For more protein, add a poached or boiled egg on top, or a spoon of rinsed chickpeas lightly mashed with the avocado. Eat straight away while the toast is crisp.

    Optional
Mash just before eating. Avocado browns quickly and toast goes soft under it. Toast, mash and assemble in one go, then eat.

Why it's healthy

Avocado and olive oil are rich in monounsaturated fat, which supports healthy LDL cholesterol, and wholegrain bread and seeds add fibre. There is no butter, cheese or processed meat. Half an avocado per slice is a good portion.

Shopping in Australia

Hass avocados are in shops all year; Shepard avocados (February to April) are great too and brown more slowly. A ripe one gives slightly when pressed near the stem. Wholegrain or seeded sourdough is at Bakers Delight, Woolworths and Coles, and pepitas are in the health food aisle.
