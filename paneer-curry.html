Paneer Curry

A mild, creamy tomato curry with soft paneer cubes, made for kids

Serves two kids · One pan

Ingredients

  • Paneer, in small cubes200 g
  • Oil or butter1 tbsp
  • Cumin seeds½ tsp
  • Onion, chopped1
  • Garlic cloves2
  • Fresh ginger1 cm piece
  • Ripe tomatoes, chopped3
  • Cashews10
    make the gravy smooth and creamy
  • Turmeric¼ tsp
  • Kashmiri chilli powder¼ tsp
    for colour, not heat; skip if your kid dislikes spice
  • Coriander powder½ tsp
  • Garam masalaa pinch
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Sugar or honey½ tsp
    balances the tomato; kids like it slightly sweet
  • Water¾ cup
  • Kasuri methi or coriander leaves1 tsp
    optional

The secret: soak the paneer in warm water while you make the gravy. It stays soft instead of turning rubbery.

Method

  1. I.

    Soften the paneer

    Put the paneer cubes in a bowl of warm salted water and leave them while you cook.

  2. II.

    Cook the base

    Heat oil in a pan. Add cumin seeds, then onion, garlic and ginger. Cook 5 minutes until soft. Add tomatoes and cashews and cook 6–7 minutes until the tomatoes are mushy.

  3. III.

    Blend smooth

    Let it cool for a few minutes first.Then blend to a smooth paste in your mixer. Kids usually prefer a gravy with no onion pieces.
  4. IV.

    Simmer the gravy

    Pour the paste back into the pan. Add turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder, salt, sugar and ¾ cup water. Simmer 5 minutes until it thickens.

  5. V.

    Add paneer

    Drain the paneer and add it to the gravy. Simmer only 2–3 minutes. Longer makes it tough.

  6. VI.

    Finish

    Stir in garam masala and crushed kasuri methi or coriander. Taste and adjust salt.

Good to know

Serve with: roti, paratha or rice.

Golden and soft: fry the paneer cubes lightly in 1 tsp oil, then drop them into warm water until the gravy is ready. This restaurant trick gives you golden edges and a soft inside.

Tofu instead of paneer: use 200 g firm tofu. Press it between paper towels with a weight on top for 10 minutes, cube it, then pan-fry in 1 tsp oil until golden. Skip the warm water soak and simmer it in the gravy for 5 minutes so it takes in the flavour.

Both in one go: make the gravy, split it into two pans, and add paneer to one and tofu to the other.

Add veg: stir in green peas or finely chopped capsicum with the paneer.

Leftovers: keeps 2 days in the fridge. Add a splash of water when reheating.

Time: about 25 minutes.
