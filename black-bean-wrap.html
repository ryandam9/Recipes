Black Bean Veggie Wrap

A wholemeal tortilla packed with cumin-warmed black beans, sweet corn, crisp lettuce, tomato and avocado, with a lime and light Greek yogurt sauce. Toasted in a dry pan so it holds together.

Method

Six steps, one frying pan and a bowl.

  1. 1

    Warm the beans and corn

    Heat the olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat. Add the drained black beans, corn, cumin, chilli flakes and a pinch of salt. Stir for 2 to 3 minutes until hot, lightly pressing a few beans with the spoon so they hold together in the wrap. Tip into a bowl and wipe the pan.

    3 minutes
  2. 2

    Make the yogurt sauce

    Stir the light Greek yogurt with the lime juice, grated garlic, a pinch of salt and pepper. Loosen with a teaspoon of water if it's very thick.

    1 minute
  3. 3

    Season the salad

    Toss the tomato, avocado, red onion and coriander with the rest of the lime juice, salt and pepper. Keep the lettuce separate so it stays crisp.

    2 minutes
  4. 4

    Soften the tortilla

    Warm the wholemeal tortilla in the dry pan for about 20 seconds a side, just until soft and pliable. A cold tortilla cracks when you roll it.

    40 seconds
  5. 5

    Fill and roll

    Spread the yogurt sauce down the middle, leaving a border. Layer the lettuce, then the beans and corn, then the tomato and avocado. Fold in the two sides, then roll up tightly from the bottom, tucking the filling in as you go.

    2 minutes
  6. 6

    Toast and cut

    Put the wrap seam-side down in the dry pan over medium heat and press it gently with a spatula. Toast for 1 to 2 minutes a side until golden and sealed. Cut in half on the diagonal.

    3 minutes
Dry the beans and lettuce well. Rinse the canned beans, then shake them dry, and pat washed lettuce with a tea towel. Extra water is what makes a wrap soggy and split.

Why it's healthy

Black beans give plant protein and plenty of fibre, which keeps you full for hours. Avocado and olive oil add heart-healthy fats, and the yogurt sauce replaces sour cream or mayonnaise. There is no cheese, butter or fried filling.

Packing it for lunch

Skip the toasting, wrap it tightly in baking paper and keep it in the fridge. Add the avocado just before eating if you can, or toss it well in lime juice so it doesn't brown. Leftover beans keep for 3 days in the fridge.
