Corn Masala

Street-style spiced corn cups, tangy and hot, ready in 10 minutes

Serves two · Snack

Ingredients

  • Fresh corn cobs2
    about 1½ cups of kernels; frozen corn works too
  • Olive oil1 tsp
  • Chaat masala½ tsp
    or roasted cumin powder with a pinch of amchur
  • Red chilli powder¼ tsp
  • Black peppera pinch
  • Salt¼ tsp
    chaat masala is salty, so taste first
  • Lemon½
  • Coriander leaves, chopped1 tbsp

The secret: toss the corn while it's still hot so the spices and lemon soak in.

Method

  1. I.

    Cut the corn

    Stand each cob upright on a board and slice the kernels off with a knife.

  2. II.

    Boil

    Boil the kernels in water with a pinch of salt for 4–5 minutes until tender. Drain well.

  3. III.

    Toss hot

    Put the hot corn in a bowl. Add olive oil, chaat masala, chilli powder, pepper and salt. Mix well.

  4. IV.

    Finish and serve

    Add lemon just before eating.Squeeze it over, sprinkle coriander and serve warm in small cups with a spoon.

Make it different

Corn chaat: let the corn cool, then add finely chopped onion, tomato, cucumber and green chilli.

Smoky: dry-roast the boiled corn in a hot pan for 2–3 minutes until a few kernels brown.

Crunchy: top with a spoon of roasted peanuts.

Time: about 10 minutes.
