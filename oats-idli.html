Oats Idli

Soft steamed idlis made from roasted oats and curd, with no grinding of rice or dal and no fermenting

Makes about twelve · Multicooker steam

Ingredients

  • Rolled oats1 cup
  • Plain curd (yoghurt)¾ cup
    slightly sour curd tastes best
  • Water¼–½ cup
    add slowly
  • Oil2 tsp
    plus a little for greasing
  • Mustard seeds½ tsp
  • Urad dal1 tsp
  • Chana dal1 tsp
  • Curry leaves, chopped8
  • Green chilli, finely chopped1
  • Fresh ginger, grated1 cm piece
  • Carrot, finely grated1 small
  • Coriander leaves, chopped2 tbsp
  • Salt½ tsp
  • Baking soda¼ tsp
    this makes them fluffy

The secret: add the baking soda right before steaming. Once it's in, the batter starts to rise, so don't let it sit.

Method

  1. I.

    Roast and grind the oats

    Dry-roast the oats in a pan for 3–4 minutes until lightly golden. Let them cool, then grind to a fine powder in your mixer.

  2. II.

    Make the tadka

    Heat 2 tsp oil. Add mustard seeds. When they pop, add urad dal and chana dal and fry until golden. Add curry leaves, green chilli and ginger, then the grated carrot. Stir 1 minute and turn off.

  3. III.

    Make the batter

    In a bowl, mix the oat powder, curd, salt, the tadka and coriander. Add water little by little until it's a thick batter, like regular idli batter: it should drop slowly off a spoon.

  4. IV.

    Rest

    Cover and leave for 10 minutes. The oats soak up liquid and the batter thickens. If it gets too stiff, add a spoon of water.

  5. V.

    Get the steamer ready

    Pour 2 cups water into the multicooker pot and set it to Steam so the water starts heating. Lightly grease your idli plates.

  6. VI.

    Add soda and fill

    Only now, add the baking soda.Sprinkle it over the batter with 1 tsp water and mix gently. The batter will turn light and bubbly. Fill the idli moulds about three-quarters full straight away.
  7. VII.

    Steam

    Place the plates in the cooker, close the lid (valve open, not sealed) and steam for 12–15 minutes. A toothpick pushed in should come out clean.

  8. VIII.

    Rest and unmould

    Leave them for 3 minutes, then loosen each idli with a wet spoon and lift out.

Good to know

Serve with: sambar, kobbari pachadi or tomato chutney.

No idli stand? Steam the batter in small greased steel bowls in the multicooker's steam basket. Larger bowls need 18–20 minutes.

Softer idlis: add 2 tbsp semolina (rava) to the batter.

Time: about 35 minutes, including resting.
