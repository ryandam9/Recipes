Rajma Curry

Soft kidney beans in a thick onion-tomato gravy

Serves four · Multicooker

Ingredients

  • Red kidney beans, soaked1 cup dry
    soaked overnight, drained and rinsed
  • Water3 cups
  • Oil1½ tbsp
    olive or sunflower
  • Cumin seeds1 tsp
  • Bay leaf1
  • Onions, finely chopped2 medium
  • Ginger, grated2 cm piece
  • Garlic cloves, crushed4
  • Green chillies, slit2
  • Tomatoes, chopped3 medium
    ripe and soft gives a better gravy
  • Turmeric½ tsp
  • Red chilli powder1 tsp
  • Coriander powder2 tsp
  • Garam masala½ tsp
  • Salt1½ tsp
  • Coriander leaves, chopped2 tbsp
  • Lemon½

The secret: mash a few beans at the end. It makes the gravy thick and creamy.

Method

  1. I.

    Fry the spices

    Set the cooker to Saute. Heat the oil, then add cumin and bay leaf. When the cumin sizzles, add the onions.

  2. II.

    Brown the onions

    Cook the onions for 8–10 minutes until golden brown. Take your time here. Add ginger, garlic and green chillies and stir for 1 minute.

  3. III.

    Make the masala

    Add tomatoes, turmeric, chilli powder, coriander powder and salt. Cook for 5–6 minutes, mashing the tomatoes, until soft and the oil starts to show at the edges.

  4. IV.

    Pressure cook the beans

    Add the soaked beans and 3 cups water. Stir and scrape the bottom.

    Don't use Stew or slow cook for raw kidney beans.They must reach full pressure or a hard boil to be safe to eat. Lock the lid, close the valve and cook on Manual, high pressure, 30 minutes. Let the pressure drop on its own, about 15 minutes.
  5. V.

    Thicken

    Open the lid and check that a bean squashes easily. Mash a spoonful of beans against the side of the pot. Switch to Saute and simmer for 5 minutes until the gravy is thick.

  6. VI.

    Finish

    Stir in garam masala, then turn off. Remove the bay leaf. Add coriander leaves and a squeeze of lemon.

Good to know

Serve with: roti, chapati or a fresh flatbread.

On the stovetop: boil the beans hard in fresh water for 10 minutes, then simmer about 1 hour until soft. Add them to the masala and simmer 15 minutes.

Time: about 1 hour, 25 minutes of it hands-on.

Leftovers: tastes even better the next day. Keeps 4 days in the fridge or 3 months frozen.
