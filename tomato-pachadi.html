Tomato Pachadi

Tomatoes cooked down with green chillies, ground with fresh coriander and finished with a crackle of mustard seeds and curry leaves. Tangy, spicy and ready in about 20 minutes.

20 minTotal time
EasySkill level
MediumHeat, from green chillies
4Servings

Ingredients

Tick things off as you gather them.

Servings
4

Method

Four steps, one pan, a blender and a small tempering pan.

  1. 1

    Fry the chillies and cumin

    Heat the 1 tsp oil in a pan. Add the cumin seeds and green chillies and fry until the cumin darkens slightly and smells toasty.

    About 30 seconds
  2. 2

    Cook the tomatoes down

    Add the tomatoes, turmeric and salt. Cover and cook on medium, stirring now and then, until the tomatoes collapse and most of their liquid has cooked off. Turn off the heat and stir in the coriander.

    About 8–10 minutes
  3. 3

    Grind coarsely

    Let the mixture cool, then pulse it in a blender to a coarse, spoonable chutney. Don't add water: the tomatoes bring all the moisture it needs.

    Cool 5 minutes first
  4. 4

    Temper and pour

    Heat the 1 tbsp oil in a small pan. Add the mustard seeds; when they splutter, add the dried red chillies and curry leaves. Pour it all over the pachadi and mix.

Cool before you blend. Hot tomatoes in a sealed blender jar build up steam and can push the lid off. Five minutes on the bench is enough.

How to serve it

Mix it into hot rice with a little ghee, or serve it with dosa, idli or chapati. Taste and adjust the salt before serving.

Quick version, no blender

Skip step 3. Mash the cooked tomatoes with the back of a spoon, stir in finely chopped coriander, then temper as usual.
