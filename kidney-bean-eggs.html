Kidney Bean Eggs

Eggs gently cooked in a spiced tomato and bean sauce, all on the stovetop

Serves four to six

Ingredients

  • Red kidney beans, dry375 g
    one packet, soaked overnight
  • Olive oil1 tbsp
  • Onion, chopped1
  • Red capsicum, in chunks1
  • Garlic cloves, crushed2
  • Ground cumin2 tsp
  • Tomatoes, roughly chopped1 kg
    about 8 medium tomatoes
  • Coriander, chopped2 tbsp + ⅓ cup
    some for the sauce, the rest for the top
  • Eggs6
  • Green chilli, sliced1
  • Feta, crumbled50–100 g
    optional
  • Salt and pepperto taste

The secret: a lid on the pan steams the eggs from the top, so you don't need an oven.

Method

  1. I.

    Cook the beans

    Drain and rinse the soaked beans. Put them in the multicooker and cover with fresh water by about 5 cm.

    Never use Stew or slow cook for raw kidney beans.Cook on Manual, high pressure, 25 minutes, then let the pressure drop on its own. Or boil hard on the stove for 10 minutes, then simmer about 1 hour until soft. Drain.
  2. II.

    Soften the vegetables

    Heat oil in a large frying pan that has a lid, on medium heat. Cook onion and capsicum for 5 minutes until soft. Add garlic and cumin and stir for 1 minute.

  3. III.

    Make the sauce

    Add tomatoes and the cooked beans. Bring to the boil, then simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens a little. Add salt, pepper and 2 tbsp coriander.

  4. IV.

    Cook the eggs

    Make 6 small hollows in the sauce with a spoon. Crack each egg into a cup, then slide it into a hollow. Sprinkle the chilli on top. Turn the heat to low and put the lid on. Cook for 6–8 minutes, until the whites are firm and the yolks are just starting to set.

  5. V.

    Serve

    Top with feta, if using, and the rest of the coriander. Eat straight from the pan with roti or bread.

Good to know

Pan too small? Cook the eggs in two batches, or use 4 eggs and keep the extra sauce.

Make ahead: the bean sauce keeps 4 days in the fridge. Warm a portion and add fresh eggs when you're ready to eat.

Time: about 1 hour 15 minutes, mostly bean cooking. Using beans you cooked earlier, it takes 25 minutes.
